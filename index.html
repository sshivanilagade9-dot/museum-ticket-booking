About this project

Online Museum Ticket Booking System for Pune

This website lets visitors search Pune museums, view timings and ticket prices, book tickets, add transport and pay online, all in one place.

Main partsUser, Museum, Ticket Booking, Transport, Payment
RelationshipsSearch, Issues, Includes, Confirms
FrontendHTML, CSS, JavaScript (React in the full version)
BackendSpring Boot, PostgreSQL (full version)
How booking works

1. Create an account and log in. 2. Search for a museum and open its details. 3. Choose the date, ticket type, number of tickets and transport. 4. Pay and receive your ticket ID.
